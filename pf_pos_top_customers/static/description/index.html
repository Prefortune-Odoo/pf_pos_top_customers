Complete Workflow for POS Top Customers & Sales Analytics

Enable the Top POS Customers Report option for selected users from settings. Open the POS Top Customers report menu and apply filters like From Date, To Date, Minimum POS Amount, Company, and POS Configuration to generate accurate customer sales reports. The module automatically displays top customers by sales based on purchase totals and selected limits. Users can also compare customer sales performance between different periods using the Compare Report option and export reports in PDF/XLS formats for POS customer reporting and sales analysis.


POS Top Customers & Sales Analytics

1. Open the user form view and enable the POS Top Customer Report option to grant report access permissions to users.

2. Go to POS → Reporting → Top Customer to access detailed customer analytics and reporting options.

3. When the user clicks on the Top POS Customers Report menu, a new reporting page opens containing two sections: Basic Report and Compare Report. In the Basic Report section, users can enter filters such as From Date, To Date, Minimum POS Amount, Company, POS Configuration, and the number of Items to display in the report. The system then analyzes all POS orders within the selected date range and checks customers who meet all the specified conditions.

4.Create a professional PDF report containing detailed customer sales analysis and purchasing insights.

5.Export the customer analytics report in XLS format for advanced data review and business analysis.

6.Modify the report settings and enable compare mode to analyze customer performance across different periods.

7.Generate a comparative PDF report to evaluate customer sales trends and performance differences between selected periods.

8.Download the comparative customer analysis report in XLS format for detailed comparison and reporting purposes.
